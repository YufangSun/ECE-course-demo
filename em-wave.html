EM Wave Propagation

A changing E field creates an H field — a changing H field creates an E field. This mutual induction lets the pair propagate through space at the speed of light, carrying energy in the direction S = E × H.

Uniform plane wave in a lossless medium:
E(z,t) = E₀ cos(ωt − kz) x̂  ·  H(z,t) = (E₀/η) cos(ωt − kz) ŷ  ·  S = E × H (W/m²)
The ratio E/H = η = √(μ/ε) is the intrinsic impedance (377 Ω in free space). In a lossy medium the wave decays as e−αz and the fields fall out of phase. At a boundary, the reflection coefficient is Γ = (η₂ − η₁)/(η₂ + η₁).
