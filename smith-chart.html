Smith chart fundamentals:
Γ = (Z_L − Z₀)/(Z_L + Z₀) maps every impedance Z_L to a point inside the unit disc. Normalizing as z = Z_L/Z₀ = r + jx, circles of constant r and arcs of constant x tile the chart. Moving a distance d toward the generator rotates Γ clockwise by 2βd (one full turn = λ/2). The VSWR is read directly from where the constant-|Γ| circle crosses the real axis: VSWR = (1+|Γ|)/(1−|Γ|).
