How the Smith Chart Is Built

The Smith chart is what you get when you apply the bilinear map Γ = (z − 1)/(z + 1) to the right-half z-plane. Vertical lines become R circles, horizontal lines become X arcs, and together they tile the unit disc.

Why a bilinear map is perfect for this: bilinear transformations send circles and straight lines in the source plane to circles and straight lines in the target plane. The imaginary axis r = 0 of the z-plane maps to the unit circle |Γ| = 1, so every passive impedance (r ≥ 0) lands inside or on that circle — which is why the whole chart fits in a finite disc.
